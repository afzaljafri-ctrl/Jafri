The initiative

Building a scalable transit payments business.

At Airtel Payments Bank, I worked across product strategy, institutional engagement and tender groundwork to develop digital transit payment opportunities in India’s metro ecosystem.

From strategy to operating proposition

Developed the bank’s NCMC strategy across prepaid and account-based ticketing, working on metro and transit RFPs and coordinating with government and institutional stakeholders. The objective was to position the bank as a credible participant in interoperable, contactless and digital transit payments.

Building the business line

Created the online and mobile smart-card recharge line of business for DMRC at Airtel Payments Bank and drove the OSR-01/2021 tender groundwork and submission. The Delhi Metro LOA was issued after my tenure; the work I led was the originating business-line development and bid groundwork.

Key projects & contributions

Institutional work across metro networks.

01 · DMRC

Smart-card recharge LOB & OSR-01/2021

Created the online/mobile smart-card recharge line of business at Airtel Payments Bank and drove institutional engagement, tender preparation and bid submission groundwork.

02 · GMRC Surat Metro

NCMC EMV-based AFC tender

Participated in the tender through an Airtel Payments Bank–PayCraft Solutions consortium, with Airtel identified as the lead member in the submitted technical bid.

03 · Mumbai Metro

QR-based payment acceptance

Introduced QR-based payment acceptance at Mumbai Metro stations, supporting a digital and cashless commuter payment experience.

04 · DMRC AFC DS24

Phase IV & network-upgrade opportunity

Reviewed tender documentation for an AFC opportunity with an approximate NIT cost of ₹411.61 crore inclusive of taxes and duties. This is described as a documentation review, not an awarded contract.
