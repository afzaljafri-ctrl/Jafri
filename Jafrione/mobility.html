The initiative

Digitising the mobility payment journey.

My work has spanned the operating side of mobility and the payments layer that supports booking, fulfilment, collections and customer journeys.

Platform integrations & payment partnerships

At Airtel Payments Bank, signed reverse-integration partnerships with Ola, Uber, RedBus and IRCTC, adding approximately ₹5 crore in GMV. The work connected mobility and travel platforms with the bank’s digital payment ecosystem.

Mobility operations foundation

Held senior operations roles at Meru Cabs across multiple tenures, including North India leadership. Managed city dispatch, fleet utilisation, collections, allocation and airport operations, and led teams of 15 operations leaders and 200+ staff in a later regional role.
