Taking FASTag beyond tolls — building a B2B parking payments business.

I introduced the B2B FASTag concept at Airtel Payments Bank and worked to extend FASTag into organised parking. The proposition connected parking technology partners, digital parking collections and the bank’s acquiring capabilities — creating a partner-led model for parking sites and malls.

The opportunity

Make parking payments as seamless as toll payments.

The idea was to take a familiar digital payment instrument and apply it to another high-frequency mobility use case: entry, exit and payment at organised parking locations.

From FASTag to parking

FASTag was already familiar to motorists on highways. The B2B proposition was to extend its use to parking environments, allowing participating facilities to digitise parking fee collection and reduce dependence on cash-based processes.

A bank-led commercial opportunity

For Airtel Payments Bank, the opportunity was not limited to enabling a payment at the barrier. It also created an acquiring opportunity with parking operators, parking locations and malls — bringing merchant collections into the bank’s payments ecosystem.

Business model

Three connected parts of the ecosystem.

A partner-led model linking the vehicle, the parking operator and the bank’s payment acceptance infrastructure.

01 · Partner platform

Parking technology integration

Work with parking technology and mobility platforms to connect FASTag-enabled vehicles with participating parking facilities and their operating systems.

02 · Digital collection

FASTag-based parking payments

Digitise parking fee collection by enabling FASTag as a payment method for eligible parking transactions, supporting smoother and more cashless customer journeys.

03 · Bank acquiring

Merchant acceptance at sites and malls

Develop the acquiring opportunity with parking operators, parking sites and malls so parking collections could flow through the bank’s merchant payments business.

Business impact

Why the initiative mattered.

The initiative positioned FASTag as more than a highway toll instrument — it became part of a broader digital mobility and merchant payments opportunity.

Expanded FASTag use cases

Extended the proposition into organised parking, adding a new mobility payment context beyond conventional toll transactions.

Digitised parking payments

Enabled a path for parking operators and facilities to collect parking fees digitally through FASTag-based payment journeys.

Built a partner-led ecosystem
